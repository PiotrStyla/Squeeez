**"What's the Hutter Prize?"**

**"How did you do it?"**
> Systematically: downloaded world-record tools (STARLIT), decomposed the gap into techniques, tested incrementally. Key discovery: effects scale non-linearly!

**"Will you beat the record?"**

**"What's non-linear scaling?"**
> When benefits grow faster than dataset size. A technique showing 2% on small data might show 30% on large data. The relationship isn't proportional - it's exponential-like. This is because patterns/redundancies only become exploitable at scale.
